## thinking-orbs

`vendor/engine.js` and `vendor/index-B8WsUNf5.js` are copied **without modification** from the public npm package
`thinking-orbs@0.3.2` (files `dist/engine.es.js` and `dist/index-B8WsUNf5.js`), by way of the local
thinking-orbs-vue port (2026-09-26).

- Upstream: https://github.com/Jakubantalik/Libraries.dev/tree/main/packages/thinking-orbs
- Published archive: https://registry.npmjs.org/thinking-orbs/-/thinking-orbs-0.3.2.tgz
- SHA-256 of engine.js: `e7dc939abf68eb2890f1a3ed49f129185e04ca690dee905b05820ed27adbf209`
- SHA-256 of index-B8WsUNf5.js: `4b43963f6409d310b9d80e592d4fb0f1435884a59d0c252f6f1b75926c09b949`
- `vendor/engine.d.ts`, `vendor/scale.js`, `vendor/scale.d.ts` are local adapters (types; re-export of the
  upstream density / radius scalers from the unmodified chunk).
- `ThinkingOrb.vue` ports the rendering steps of upstream's React `ThinkingOrb`; HiTK adds arbitrary pixel sizes
  and a decorative default.
- `organic-orbits.ts` adds a local working-state variation with independently sized particles, varied speeds,
  and drifting elliptical paths, using the upstream engine for the faint background orbits and painting.
- `public/logo.svg` is one frame of the "connecting" mode, generated by `gen-logo.mjs` from the engine above.
